FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know.

Is Resux really free?
Yes. Building, editing, PDF export and the online portfolio are all free with no watermark. We believe job-hunting tools should be accessible to everyone.
Where is my data stored?
Your resume is saved only in your own browser (localStorage). If you publish a portfolio, its data is stored on the server only to render that link. No email, no signup.
How good is the PDF?
The output is fully vector: text stays selectable and searchable, stays sharp at any zoom and typically weighs under 200 KB. Ideal for print and email.
What is ATS and why does it matter?
ATS (Applicant Tracking System) is software companies use to scan resumes first. Our templates are simple and standard so these systems read your text correctly.
How does the portfolio link work?
Click “Publish online” to get a short link (like resux.jx3.ir/u/abc123) plus a secret edit key. Use the key to edit that link anytime. Links unvisited for 6 months are removed automatically.
What if I close the tab?
Nothing is lost — every keystroke is auto-saved in your browser. You can also export a JSON backup and re-upload it anytime.
